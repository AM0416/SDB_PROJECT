# Publication Policy

**Document ID:** SDB-POLICY-001  
**Version:** v1.0.0  
**Status:** READY FOR RELEASE

---

## Purpose

This repository publishes documented observations and the research artifacts derived from them.

Its purpose is to preserve transparency throughout the process by documenting observations, their interpretation, and the artifacts generated from those observations.

The repository does not seek to establish universal theories or present definitive conclusions.

Instead, it provides a structured record of observations and the materials created from those observations.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to every document, figure, dataset, and supplementary material published within the SDB_PROJECT repository.

It defines:

- publication principles
- source hierarchy
- transparency requirements
- review requirements
- release workflow
- version management
- responsibility for publication

---

## Publication Principles

### Observation First

Observed events remain primary.

Interpretations, analyses, and research outputs are secondary artifacts derived from documented observations.

Primary observations are not replaced by later interpretations.

---

### Transparency

Published documents should clearly distinguish between:

- Observation
- Interpretation
- Analysis
- Discussion
- Hypothesis

These categories should not be merged without explicit explanation.

---

### Open Conclusions

The repository does not present documented observations as universal proof.

Readers may examine the published material and develop their own interpretations.

Conclusions remain open.
