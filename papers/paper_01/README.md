# Paper 01

**Working Title**

The Temperature and Humidity of Words

---

## Overview

This directory contains the first research paper published as part of the SDB_PROJECT.

The paper examines observations derived from Real-Time Narrative Records (RTNRs) and explores how subtle characteristics of language—described metaphorically as "temperature" and "humidity"—may influence interpretation during human–AI interaction.

The objective is not to establish universal theory, but to document observations and present the research artifacts derived from them.
