# Paper 02

**Working Title**

Externalized Human–AI Model Revision

---

## Overview

This directory contains the second research paper published as part of SDB_PROJECT.

The paper examines a recurring interactional process in which an AI system presents a provisional representation of its understanding, the human participant inspects that representation, and corrections are used to revise the working model.

The study focuses on observable interaction rather than hidden AI reasoning.

Its central concern is the timing and structure of interpretation during continuing human–AI dialogue.

---

## Central Observation

Across the recorded dialogue, the AI frequently moved too quickly from observation to interpretation.

The human participant repeatedly redirected the process by correcting:

- processing order
- conceptual placement
- category assignment
- structural organization
- premature completion of incomplete meaning

The observable interaction can be provisionally represented as:

```text
Observation
    ↓
AI Rendering
    ↓
External Representation
    ↓
Human Inspection
    ↓
Correction
    ↓
AI Revision
    ↓
Verification
