# SDB_PROJECT

> A public repository of observations derived from Real-Time Narrative Records (RTNRs).

---

## Purpose

SDB_PROJECT is a public repository dedicated to publishing observations derived from Real-Time Narrative Records (RTNRs).

The purpose of this repository is not to establish universal theories or persuade readers to adopt predetermined conclusions.

Instead, it documents observations and publishes research artifacts generated from those observations.

Readers are invited to examine the published records and develop their own interpretations.

---

## Core Principles

This repository distinguishes clearly between:

- Real-Time Narrative Records (RTNRs)
- Observation Records
- Case Studies
- Research Reports
- Operational Records

Observations remain primary.

Interpretations are documented separately.

Research reports organize observations but do not replace the original narrative records.

---

## AI Collaboration

This repository is produced through collaboration between a human author and ChatGPT.

AI participation is disclosed rather than concealed.

Published materials may therefore contain AI-generated text, AI-assisted editing, and AI-assisted analytical organization.

The responsibility for publication remains with the human author.

---

## Continuous Context

The observations published here were generated within a continuously used ChatGPT environment.

Accordingly, AI responses were produced within an accumulated conversational context rather than a newly initialized session.

This contextual environment is disclosed as part of the documented conditions under which the observations were made.
