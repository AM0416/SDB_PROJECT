## Purpose

The Real-Time Narrative Record (RTNR) is the primary source document of the SDB_PROJECT.

Its purpose is to preserve observations as they occurred, before later interpretation, restructuring, or analysis.

RTNR provides the foundation from which all subsequent research artifacts are derived.

---

## Definition

A Real-Time Narrative Record is a chronological record produced during ongoing human–AI interaction.

It captures the progression of dialogue, observations, decisions, and contextual changes as they naturally unfold.

An RTNR is not a summary or retrospective reconstruction.

It is a contemporaneous narrative record.
