## Conflict and Correction Policy

When a conflict exists between AI-generated material and the human author's confirmed record:

1. the confirmed human record takes priority
2. the conflicting AI-generated statement is excluded from further use
3. the correction is documented when relevant
4. derived artifacts are reviewed for the same error

Unconfirmed information remains unconfirmed.
