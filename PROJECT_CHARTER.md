# Purpose

SDB_PROJECT is a public repository for documenting and publishing observations derived from Real-Time Narrative Records (RTNRs).

The purpose of this repository is to preserve observations and publish research artifacts generated from those observations.

This project does not begin from predetermined conclusions.

It begins from documented observations.

---

# Mission

The mission of SDB_PROJECT is to preserve observations faithfully, distinguish observations from interpretations, and publish research artifacts with transparency regarding their origin and production process.

---

# Primary Source

The primary source of this repository is the **Real-Time Narrative Record (RTNR).**

Every publication hosted by this repository originates from one or more RTNRs.

Subsequent documents organize observations but do not replace the original narrative record.

---

# Research Position

The repository does not seek to establish universal theories.

Instead, it documents phenomena observed within their recorded context.

Readers are encouraged to examine published observations independently.

Alternative interpretations remain possible.

---

# Observation First

The repository distinguishes between:

- Real-Time Narrative Records
- Observation Records
- Interpretations
- Analytical Models
- Case Studies
- Research Reports
- Operational Records

These categories are documented separately whenever possible.

---

# Human–AI Collaboration

Publications in this repository are produced through collaboration between a human author and ChatGPT.

ChatGPT contributes to dialogue, structural organization, editorial refinement, comparative analysis, and publication preparation.

The participation of AI is disclosed rather than concealed.

---

# Continuous Context

Observations published in this repository originate from a continuously used ChatGPT environment.

The AI responses documented here were produced within accumulated conversational context, project-specific documentation, and ongoing interaction.

This contextual environment is treated as part of the documented observational conditions.

---

# Human Responsibility

The human author remains responsible for:

- selecting observations;
- organizing publications;
- distinguishing observation from interpretation;
- determining publication scope;
- approving revisions;
- deciding what is released publicly.

AI participation does not transfer publication responsibility.

---

# Transparency

Transparency is a publication principle.

Accordingly, this repository discloses:

- AI collaboration;
- accumulated conversational context;
- document version;
- document status;
- publication workflow.

---

# Publication Philosophy

This repository publishes observations.

Interpretations are documented.

AI participation is disclosed.

Conclusions remain open.

Readers are invited to examine the observations and determine their own interpretations.

---

# Scope

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, publications describe observations made within their documented context.

No publication should automatically be interpreted as establishing universal properties of:

- language;
- translation;
- artificial intelligence;
- human cognition;
- communication.

---

# Closing Statement

> Observe carefully.
>
> Document faithfully.
>
> Publish transparently.
